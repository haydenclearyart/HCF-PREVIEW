Privacy
Policy.

Last updated: 24 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information Hayden Cleary Fitness collects, why it's collected, how it's used and stored, and what you can do about it. It applies to this website, the online coaching programs, the Fit Files digital guides, and any challenges run through the business.

1. Who we are

Hayden Cleary Fitness ("HCF", "we", "us", "I") is an online fitness coaching business operated by Hayden Cleary in New South Wales, Australia. ABN 62 887 108 993.

We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

2. What we collect

Depending on how you use HCF, we may collect:

  • Contact details — your name, email address and phone number, usually given when you download a free guide or enquire about coaching.
  • Health and fitness information — training history, injuries, medical conditions, medications, goals, body measurements, weight and progress photos, where you provide them as part of coaching or a pre-exercise screening.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe. HCF never sees or stores your full card number. We receive only a transaction record (amount, date, last four digits, subscription status).
  • Program and challenge activity — check-ins, submitted photos or videos, and points where you take part in a group challenge.
  • Website usage data — basic technical information such as the pages you visit and the device or browser you're using.

3. Health information is sensitive information

Under Australian privacy law, health information is treated as sensitive information and gets extra protection. We only collect it with your consent, only where it's genuinely needed to coach you safely, and we don't use it for anything else.

You can decline to provide health information. If you do, some parts of coaching may not be able to run safely, and we'll tell you where that's the case rather than guess.

4. Why we collect it

  • To deliver the coaching, guide or challenge you signed up for.
  • To screen for injury and medical risk before prescribing training.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To answer your enquiries and stay in contact about your program.
  • To send you the free guide you requested and, if you've opted in, occasional updates about HCF.
  • To meet legal, insurance and record-keeping obligations.

5. Who we share it with

HCF does not sell your personal information. We share it only with the services needed to run the business:

  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription billing.
  • Google — forms, file storage and email used to run coaching and challenges.
  • Email and form delivery providers — to send the free guide and coaching correspondence.
  • Professional advisers, insurers or authorities — only where we're required or permitted by law.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. By using HCF you consent to your information being handled overseas by these providers under their own privacy terms.

6. Progress photos and content

Progress photos, check-in videos and results are kept private by default. Nothing you send is ever posted publicly, used in marketing, or shown to another client without your specific, prior permission for that specific use.

If you've given permission and later change your mind, tell us and we'll stop using it going forward.

7. How we store and protect it

Your information is stored in password-protected accounts and cloud services with access restricted to Hayden. HCF is a solo operation — there's no team, no agency, and no third-party staff with access to your files.

No online system is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

We keep your information only as long as it's needed for the purpose it was collected, or as long as the law requires. After that it's deleted or de-identified.

8. Cookies and website data

This website may use cookies and similar technology to keep the site working properly and to understand which pages get used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

If you arrive from a social media ad or link, that platform may pass through its own tracking parameters. Those are governed by that platform's privacy policy, not this one.

9. Marketing and opting out

If you've given us your email, we may send you occasional updates about HCF programs, guides and challenges. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time by clicking it or by contacting us directly. Opting out of marketing doesn't affect emails about a program you're currently in.

10. Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct anything that's wrong, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we'll respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

There are limited situations where we may need to refuse a request — for example, where the law requires us to keep a record. If that happens, we'll tell you why in writing.

11. Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your personal information, contact us first and we'll work through it with you. If you're not satisfied with the outcome, you can take the complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the business changes. The current version always lives on this page, with the last-updated date at the top. Continuing to use HCF after a change means you accept the updated policy.
