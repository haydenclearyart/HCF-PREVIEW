About

I'm not trying to be
your coach forever.

I'm Hayden. I coach people online who've decided they want to change and don't know where to start — or have started before and stopped. My job is to make myself unnecessary.

The
short
version

I've been training for over ten years. Different gyms, different approaches, plenty of them wrong at the time.

I've been the person training six days a week without missing one. I've also been the person who didn't walk into a gym for months. Both of those are me.

That second part is the reason I coach the way I do. Starting is easy — anyone can be motivated on a Monday. Starting again, after the injury or the busy year or the burnout, is the hard one. Most coaching is built for people who are already going. Mine is built for people who've stopped.

So there's no template with your name pasted on it and no bootcamp energy. You get a program built around your week, your equipment and your actual life — and you get me on the other end of it. Not a support team, not a chatbot, not an assistant answering under my name. One person, one inbox.

I'm not interested in keeping you on the plan forever. I'd rather teach you enough that you don't need the plan.

How I coach

Four things I
actually believe.

Not a philosophy page. These are the four calls that shape every program I write.

01
You're paying for time, not features
Every package gets the same thing — the program, the nutrition guidance, the check-ins, the guides. Nothing is locked behind a higher tier. The only difference between four weeks and twelve is how long you've got to make it stick.
02
The plan bends before you break
Work goes sideways. Kids get sick. You get three hours of sleep. A program that only works on a perfect week isn't a program. Tell me what changed and we adjust it — that's the whole job.
03
You should know why, not just what
Anyone can hand you a spreadsheet of sets and reps. If you don't understand why it's built that way, you're back to guessing the day it ends. I explain the reasoning as we go, on purpose.
04
Honest beats nice
If something isn't working, I'll say so. If you've gone quiet for two weeks, I'll ask why. You're not paying me to agree with you — you're paying me to notice.
The whole idea

Hire me to fire me.

Most coaching is built to keep you subscribed. That's the business model — stay dependent, keep paying. Mine runs the other way.

The day you can build your own week, adjust your own training when life shifts, and eat without checking in with me first — that's the day the job's done. You leave, and you don't come back, because you don't need to.

That's the result I'm aiming for. Not a client for life. A person who doesn't need a coach.

Before you sign up

This works for
some people. Not all.

I'd rather you read this and decide it's not for you than pay me and find out in week two.

This is for you if
You've decided you want to change and you're not sure how to start.
You've started before and stopped — more than once.
You want to understand what you're doing, not just follow instructions.
You'll message me when things go sideways instead of going quiet.
You want a coach who tells you the truth, not one who tells you what you want to hear.
This isn't for you if
You're after a six-week shortcut with no change to your lifestyle.
You want someone to tell you what to do and nothing else.
You want a meal plan with foods you don't like and never want to eat again.
You want a permanent change but you want to be paying a coach forever to keep it.
